Bluetooth 5.2 vs 2.4 GHz Wireless Technology Comparison

🔵 Bluetooth 5.2

Advantages

  • No USB dongle required - built into most modern devices
  • Lower power consumption extends battery life
  • Can connect to multiple devices simultaneously
  • Universal standard across all devices
  • Improved security with encryption
  • Better range (up to 40 meters in open space)
  • LE Audio support for enhanced connectivity

Disadvantages

  • Slightly higher latency (10-20ms typical)
  • Can experience interference from other Bluetooth devices
  • Initial pairing process required
  • May have compatibility issues with older systems
  • Polling rate typically limited to 125-250 Hz

Best For

  • Office work and general productivity
  • Multi-device usage (laptop, tablet, phone)
  • Portable setups and travel
  • Users who want minimal desk clutter
  • Battery life priority

📡 2.4 GHz Wireless

Advantages

  • Lower latency (1-8ms typical)
  • Higher polling rates (up to 1000 Hz or more)
  • More stable connection with less interference
  • Instant connectivity - no pairing needed
  • Better performance for gaming and precision tasks
  • Works with any device with a USB port
  • Consistent performance across all scenarios

Disadvantages

  • Requires a USB dongle (occupies a USB port)
  • Higher power consumption reduces battery life
  • Limited to one device at a time
  • Can lose the USB receiver
  • Shorter effective range (typically 10 meters)

Best For

  • Gaming and competitive esports
  • Graphic design and precision work
  • Video editing and professional applications
  • Users prioritizing performance over portability
  • Situations requiring minimal input lag

💡 Our Recommendation

Choose Bluetooth 5.2 if: You value portability, multi-device connectivity, and longer battery life. Perfect for office work, casual gaming, and users who frequently switch between devices. The lack of a USB dongle keeps your setup clean and minimalist.

Choose 2.4 GHz Wireless if: You prioritize performance, low latency, and high precision. Ideal for competitive gaming, professional creative work, and scenarios where every millisecond counts. The dedicated connection ensures consistent, reliable performance.

Hybrid Solution: Many modern mice offer both connectivity options, giving you the flexibility to choose based on your current needs. Use 2.4 GHz for gaming sessions and switch to Bluetooth for travel or general use.
